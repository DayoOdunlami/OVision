## Standards

- Anything visual or animated on the pond or any room: follow the
  `abide-direction` skill (`.claude/skills/abide-direction/SKILL.md`) and run its
  director's checklist.
- Any user-facing change: run the `product-review` skill
  (`.claude/skills/product-review/SKILL.md`), apply its fixes, and say in one line what
  you cut and why.
- Check changes in a real browser (Playwright is available) at desktop and 390 px, by
  day and at `?h=22`, before pushing.

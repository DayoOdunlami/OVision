## Deliberate disagreement before a decision

- Product strategist: the one-door home should offer two obvious intentions (pray quietly; pray together), not demand a tour of rooms. The weekly verse is the common thread, not a mandatory three-activity itinerary.
- Spiritual director: even a beautiful pond behind Dad's prayer creates a second task for his eyes. Put the living world before/after the reading, keep words still and never gate prayer behind a chore.
- Children's designer: do not simplify away all-word falling and rebuilding. The shared Q&A calls the default “Memory” while saying every word falls. Resolve the behaviour, not the label: every verse word falls; names do not appear in that verse. One forgiving shared puzzle, no individual races or score.
- Behavioural designer: the weekly surprise can stay a delight without being a variable-ratio reward. Monday deterministically changes the verse; the whole household sees the same one all week. There is nothing to lose by missing a day.
- Decision recommendation: make quiet prayer the adult default, with explicit together use changing the sitting's participants; keep the puzzle as invited play after Amen and available directly. Ambient pond and memory of the season belong outside the text, so familiar craft does not fight the practice.

## Adult confession recommendation

The adult's task is honesty before God, not clearing a screen. Use one quiet prompt such as “Bring to mind a moment you withheld love. Tell God plainly.” Then an original prayer asking forgiveness and help to put something right. Offer “Stay here” / “Continue when ready” with no countdown or required answer. Do not store the content. Do not record number of sins, display moral progress, or visually award forgiveness for a tap.

A child's tangible version can be opt-in. Limit it to one chosen stone, with a plain connection between an action and the metaphor. Avoid inherited labels such as Insecurity, Doubt or Worry being counted as wrongs: ordinary feelings are not themselves sins. A repair could happen off-screen. Do not make the pre-reader the core design target against Q&A.

## Scope calls worth recording in the digest

1. Every-word fall is the expected behaviour even though Q&A calls it “Memory”; honour the stated favourite rather than preserve a misleading old label.
2. NIV source wording belongs in an immutable weekly verse object shared by prayer and puzzle. Personal names go only in the surrounding prayer. Include copyright acknowledgement and small known verse count.
3. Completion is per sitting or per participant. A morning Amen leaves evening prayer open; daily aggregate should never imply everyone prayed.
4. Existing records need not migrate; preserve an explicit schema seam rather than rush an unreliable import.
5. A family board can be made directly this round. Connected personal boards can remain backlog if the chosen design cannot make them graceful in one session.

# Tend

**A household prayer book: Scripture, held together.**

At 06:45, open Tend and choose **Pray quietly**. One more tap brings you to Scripture. The page is still, the words are large, and each thought waits for you. Pray for today's people, make room for your own response, and leave with one small way to carry it into life.

After dinner, open the same prayer together. Choose who is here. Dad's morning Amen has not finished the family's day. When you are ready, let every word of this week's verse fall and find them again: tap, drag, ask for a hint, have another go. Prayer and play use exactly the same NIV wording, without family names inserted into Scripture. A new verse arrives each Monday; Sunday holds everyone and closes the week.

We kept what earns its place: the falling words, feeding koi, warm reading typography, a living place and a longer seasonal direction. We joined the scattered rooms into Today, Our garden and Our season. We cut the builder's switches, competing worlds, six fixed people, hidden difficulty menus and rewards that mistake frequency for faithfulness.

The garden remembers people being present, never who is behind. Adult confession is a private pause for honesty, forgiveness and repair; children can lay down one stone without reporting a private fault to the screen. Our season can be reviewed together or taken to paper. No plant dies when you stay away.

Tend installs to an Android home screen and keeps its book, game, fonts and memories on this device for offline use. A fictional household makes every moment easy to explore. A new household needs only a few names.

The foundation separates household identity, Scripture and recorded sittings. Personal boards feeding a family board, a wider verse library and careful cross-device sharing have a place to grow. Accounts, payments, new disciplines and sound wait. What matters now is a place this family can choose, use, and leave well.

The digest records testing and limits honestly: these are reviewer walkthroughs, not a test with the actual children; native Android installation and the two-second target on ordinary hardware still need a device check.

The shared answers are merged and take precedence. The local base is the reference. Gate 0 inspected Pond, Pray, the puzzle, Abide, Today, Make, Flourish and Murmuration at 390 × 844 and 1180 × 820. ## Protect

- Prayer reads like a page rather than a dashboard. Deliberate type and one thought at a time are useful foundations for silent prayer.
- The puzzle is physical: words really fall, remain readable, and accept a tap as well as a drag. Identical words are interchangeable. Keep the experience, not the misleading difficulty labels.
- An inhabited landscape is a more distinctive place to return to than a completion chart. Koi feeding is play without winners.
- A season lasts longer than today's activity; the board can hold direction without becoming another daily task.

## Repair or remove

- There is no clear front door; Today is hidden behind a builder setting. The user must know rooms and URLs.
- Morning and evening currently share a global completion flag, even when different people are present.
- Names and spoken paraphrases enter the puzzle. Learning needs one immutable Scripture passage all week.
- The code's Memory cut drops every third word; the shared answer describes all words dropping as the favourite. Honour the requested behaviour.
- Household indices assume six people. New household members require stable IDs and a rota that works with other sizes.
- Confession is a metaphor with no assurance that reflection occurs. Feelings such as worry and insecurity must not become a moral score.
- Different illustrations, pages, settings and many hidden mechanics demand discovery work from the family. Labs and Admin belong outside the new family journey.
- Week rollover depends on creation time. Monday must be the boundary; Sunday closes the same week's passage.
- No manifest, complete offline path or first-run household exists.

## Independent role disagreement

The art direction favours a living place; the spiritual direction refuses movement behind silent prayer. The children's designer protects falling words and refuses an adult stillness exercise as an entrance fee to play. The behavioural direction rejects growth tied to performance or missed days. Resolution: one quiet household fieldbook; a garden outside the prayer, tangible play after or directly accessible, and a neutral record of shared sittings rather than spiritual achievement.

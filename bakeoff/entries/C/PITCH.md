# Entry C: *Quiet Waters*

**One quiet place where a household prays for each other from Scripture every day,
learns the week's verse by heart in play, and sees, in a pond that fills over the season,
who they are becoming.**

## Who it serves, and how

- **Dad, 06:45, phone, in the dark.** One tap on the home-screen icon, one on *Pray*.
  The water stills into night paper and the sitting runs in the same order every day:
  three breaths with the week's breath prayer, the verse read twice with its word lifted
  out in gold, twenty seconds of quiet, then the prayer for today's two people one
  thought at a time. When his mind wanders, *Wandered?* gives back one word to return
  with. That is Centering Prayer's oldest answer, not a nudge or a streak. Amen is held
  in silence, and then their koi rise beneath the page.
- **The children, after dinner, tablet.** The app knows they're in the room, so the
  prayer is in simple words, a different child reads each card (their koi colour shows
  whose turn), then "I wonder…" and the blessing together. Amen, feed the koi, then
  *Play the verse*: their favourite puzzle, now in four steps across the week, ending in
  *By heart*. Different words fall each time, so replaying teaches the verse, not five of
  its words.
- **Sunday, everyone.** The Lord's Prayer, each person held with their own word for the
  season, the verse said together from memory (a lotus opens on the pond), and the week
  looked back on. The new prayer arrives on Monday morning as a small surprise.
- **A glance.** Left alone, the tablet becomes the pond: the family's word in the water,
  the verse, and today's names beside their koi. It can be kept awake.

## What we kept, merged, cut and added

- **Kept:** the koi (and feeding them), the verse puzzle's feel, Pray's craft and every
  word of its fourteen prayers, Amen bringing the koi. These are the things this family
  already loves.
- **Merged:** Pond, Pray, Abide, Today and Make into **one app with one door**. Abide's
  best ideas live on: the breath prayer, reading twice, the stillness, "I wonder", the
  stones, the slow accumulation. The board became **seasons**: each person's word feeds
  the family's word, chosen together by passing the tablet round.
- **Cut:**
  - Abide's sow-grow-gather, a second daily practice in a second world.
  - The vineyard: a young one is an empty field.
  - Flourish, Murmuration, Labs and Admin.
  - The voice, person, celebration and patience settings, which are now inferred from
    who's in the room.
  - Notifications, which we never built.

  The old addresses still redirect.
- **Added:**
  - **Into the deep**, a confession that lands for an adult: reflection first, then one
    stone opened from the hand, and "He casts it into the depths". Children each throw a
    stone (sorry) or a flower (thank you).
  - Night paper.
  - A rota worked out from the date, so the phone and the tablet agree on today without
    sync.
  - Any household: set up in a minute, or carry it to another device with a link.
  - Installable, works offline, and a printed weekly card for the bedside.
  - An example family at `?demo=1`.

## Why a family would choose it every day

It asks for five minutes and gives something back each time, without ever counting
anything against anyone. There are no streaks, no scores, no notifications, and nothing
withers when you miss a day (a week away comes back to "Welcome back."). The children
come back for the koi and the puzzle. Dad comes back because it keeps his attention on
the two people in front of God instead of on the app. The pond is a different picture at
dawn, by day, at dusk and at night. It fills with lily pads and lotuses over the term, so
the family can see what has taken root.

## How it grows

- **The data model is already shaped for sync:** a household, seasons and append-only
  sittings. A small backend (Supabase, say) slots in behind `store.js` with no change to
  the rooms.
- **Accounts and other families:** setup and the device link already make any household
  work. Sign-in replaces the link.
- **New disciplines are new sittings in the same grammar** (still, Scripture, respond,
  Amen), opened from the pond:
  - an evening Examen, starting from *Into the deep*;
  - Sabbath, starting from Sunday;
  - fasting, as a season practice.
- **Sound, when wanted:** a bell to open the threshold, and the verse read aloud for
  pre-readers.
- **Licensing:** NIV for a paid product is a backlog question.

# A compass, held in a living place

**Who we are becoming. What we make room for. What we leave behind.** Three intentions, chosen for a term, which a family can carry into an ordinary decision today.

The board is the home and the daily glance, rather than a page behind the prayer. Nobody checks off a promise. The household chooses its own word and concrete yes/no, either directly or after passing the tablet to each person. Each personal board has the same grammar; its contribution visibly joins the family. The family chooses what to carry together, rather than an algorithm averaging its words.

The pond keeps the existing living koi. Each person's koi belongs to their intention; gathering, resting, rising and making space express what their word means. The grove shares a root: each person's bough helps form a crown, growth turns towards the light, and an open clearing protects the space the family has chosen to leave. It is a different natural metaphor, not fish exchanged for leaves.

A word can mean different things to different people. Six themes suggest behaviours; the person can choose another meaning. An unfamiliar word receives a gentle growth default. Nature expresses an intention, never a claim that the family has accomplished it. Seasonal development follows time, never app use.

World choice belongs to this device. A visible Pond/Grove control carries the choice through the home, personal and family boards and the visual return after Amen. Prayer stays paper; its words, puzzle and confession practice are not rewritten in this round. Confession's literal water metaphor remains part of that existing practice, rather than being silently recast as a different discipline.

We keep Quiet Waters' typography, reading surfaces, living koi and pass-the-tablet ritual. We replace the list-led board with readable anchors in a living scene, and make room for practical boundaries. We reject a sky of decorative particles, growth earned by prayer counts, daily tasks and a third world that would dilute the craft.

The initial board studies are in `tiles/`: pond and grove at phone and tablet size, committed before product code. They establish composition, not a promise to freeze the illustration before it is tested in motion.

# Style tiles (studio P)

The family board, at rest (the glance), in each world. Both sizes, by day (`h=11`) and at
night (`h=22`). They were drawn with the real code: the koi from `src/app/pond/SpineFish.js`,
and the vine word-writer from `main` (`src/flourish/lib/`), extended for the trunk,
trellis, pruning and per-person branches. They were rendered in Chromium before any
product code changed.

The invented family has six people: Sam (Steady), Jo (Present), Ada (Brave), Kit (Kind),
Noor (Joyful) and Pip (Helpful). Between them they cover all six themes.

**What to look for**
- **The word.**
  - In the pond it lies in still water.
  - In the vine the vine writes it, along the trellis wire, rising from an unnamed trunk.
- **The yeses.**
  - In the pond they are lily pads.
  - In the vine they are tags on the trellis posts.
  - Today's yes: in the pond its pad has a white water lily; in the vine a tendril has
    wound round its post and the tag is lit.
  - On a phone the pads and tags carry no words; the line above names today's.
- **The nos.**
  - In the pond, a stone rests beneath each yes it guards.
  - In the vine, each is a pruned spur with a new shoot, and the cutting lies at the
    foot.
- **Each of us.** In the vine every branch is shaped by its theme:
  - Sam, steadfast: thick.
  - Jo, rest: hangs low.
  - Ada, growing: a tendril reaching up.
  - Kit, together: twined with Noor.
  - Noor, shining: blossom along it.
  - Pip, giving: grapes.
  - Each branch ends in a blossom in its person's koi colour.
  - Ada and Kit, prayed for today, carry white flowers.
  - On a phone there are no names; on a tablet the names are still, at the branch ends.
- **No text on any fish.** The koi are only themselves.
- **Word count at rest.**
  - The pond on a phone: 18, including the word "Today" and the clock. Of these, 15 are
    the board's own words.
  - The vine on a phone: the same 15, plus the vine's word.

The tile captions in the bottom-left corner are the harness's, not the app's.

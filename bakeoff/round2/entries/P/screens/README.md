# Screens (studio P)

Every screenshot here was taken in Chromium from the running app, using the example
family (`?demo=1`). Where a filename doesn't give the time:
- **Day** is `h=11`.
- **Night** is `h=22`.
- **Dawn** is `h=6.75`, the 06:45 prayer.

**Sizes**
- Phone: `390` is 390 × 844.
- Tablet: `1180` is 1180 × 820 (landscape).

## The board in each world

Each set covers family and personal, phone and tablet, day and night.
- `board-pond-family-*`: the family's board in the pond.
- `board-vine-family-*`: the family's board on the vine.
- `board-*-person-ada-390-*` and `board-*-person-sam-1180-*`: someone's own board.
  - Ada is a child, so her board shows a yes only.
  - Sam is a grown-up. His no appears only on his own board.

## Upgrades, side by side

| Comparison | Why ours is better |
|---|---|
| `upgrade-pond-1180-before-after` | It keeps the living koi and adds what the board means. The yeses float on pads (today's has a water lily), and the no each protects rests as a stone beneath it. No fish carries words or swims across them. It is quieter: fewer drifting leaves. |
| `upgrade-pond-390-before-after` | The phone glance gives today's yes in place of the full verse, so it shows fewer words. Koi beneath the words are seen deep, through still water. |
| `upgrade-vine-1180-before-after` | It keeps the proven word-writer and adds a rooted, unnamed trunk ("I am the vine"). Each person is a branch shaped by what their word means, the yeses are a trellis, and the nos are a pruning. It stands in a real field with the clock's light and one wind. |
| `upgrade-board-390-before-after` | The season page was a record you rarely open. Now the board is the home screen, in its world, glanced every day, and the paper page is there for the whole thing. |

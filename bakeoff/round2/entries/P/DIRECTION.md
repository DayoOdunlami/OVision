# Design direction: Our Way (studio P)

Written before any product code, after the bench argued it out. The style tiles in
`tiles/` render it.

## The board, and why

**A family's way for this season: one word for who we're becoming, a few yeses, and the
no each yes protects. It lives in a world you can leave on the side.**

The four-part board (identity, season, commitments, friction) answered "who are we?" and
then gave nothing to act on today. Ours turns the word into choices.

| Part | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Our word** (and a line) | This season's answer to who we are in Christ. It is not a new identity each term. | The season and identity parts merged: one word a five-year-old can hold. |
| **Our yeses** (up to 3) | Practices and commitments: *slow dinners together*, *Sunday kept for rest*. | A yes is something you can do today. |
| **The no each yes protects** (up to 2) | Written as *no to hurry, so yes to slow dinners*. | A no exists only to guard a yes. On its own it becomes a rule list (spiritual director). |
| **Each of us** | A word, what it means (one of six pictures), and one yes. Grown-ups may add a no, kept only on their own board. | Personal boards feed the family's: the family word and yeses are chosen from what everyone brought. |

**Every day: the glance.** The board is the home screen and the idle screen. At rest it
shows three things in the world:
- the word;
- one line, *Today: yes to slow dinners together*, which rotates through the yeses and
  never names a no;
- who we pray for today.

Nothing to tick. *Weighing a decision?* is a quiet link on the slip. It asks three
questions: does it help us be *Rooted*? does it make room for one of our yeses? which
way leaves us more loving and more at peace? It saves nothing.

**Every term: the ritual.** Pass the tablet:
1. **Look back.** *Where did we see God this term?* Last season's word is shown; nothing
   is marked kept or broken.
2. **Each person in turn.**
   - Their own creature first ("this is you").
   - What their word means, tapped as a picture.
   - The word, offered as words that fit that picture (a child taps, an older one types).
   - One yes.
3. **Together.** Our word, our yeses and the no each one protects, chosen from what
   everyone brought.
4. **Where the board lives:** the pond or the vine.
5. **The finale.**
   - Each person arrives in the world one at a time, named as they arrive.
   - The youngest child taps to let the family word in.
   - Each no is let go: a stone sinks in the pond, or a cutting falls from the vine.

## The world contract

Every world must say these things, each in its own way.

| Meaning | The pond | The vine |
|---|---|---|
| **Our word** | Lies in the still water at the centre. Every few minutes the koi gather round its edge, never over it. | Written by the vine itself as it grows, along the trellis wire. |
| **Who we're becoming** (the word's theme) | Sets how the koi gather: resting together, circling, fanning out, rising into the light. | Sets what the vine bears: jasmine, blossom, more leaf, or grapes. |
| **Each of us** | A koi per person, in their own variety, moving by their word's theme. Tap one to see its name and word on still water. | A branch per person, abiding in the vine and shaped by their theme. It ends in a blossom in their colour; the name shows on a tablet, or on a tap. |
| **Our yeses** | Lily pads that carry the yes words. Koi rest under them and rock them. | Tags on the trellis posts: the rule of life holding the vine up. |
| **Today's yes** | Its pad opens a water lily. | A tendril reaches out and curls round its post. |
| **The no that protects a yes** | A stone resting on the floor beneath its yes. It sank once, in the ritual, and the water above is clear. | A pruned cutting lying at the vine's foot, where the cut put out a new shoot (John 15:2). |
| **This week's verse** | A line on the water (on a phone, the reference). | A line in the sky (on a phone, the reference). |
| **Who we pray for today** | Their names on the water. Their koi swim as a pair. | Their names in the sky. Their branches carry white blossom. |
| **What has grown** | A small pad for every week of the season, and a water lily on each week whose verse was said by heart. Nothing withers. | The vine leafs out week by week, and its grapes ripen as the season goes. A blossom marks each verse said by heart. Nothing withers. |
| **Calm when praying** | Paper over a paused world, in the pond's palette. | Paper over a paused world, in the vine's linen palette. |
| **Being known** (touch) | Touch your koi: it turns to your finger, and its name rests on the water. | Touch your branch: it lifts towards you, its leaves ruffle, and its name rests in the air. |

**The attention budget**, after the bench:
- **Words at rest:** 12 at most on a phone and 20 on a tablet, counted from the
  screenshots.
- **Moving labels:** none. No text on koi or on branches.
- **Moving systems:** at most two besides the water's current or the one wind.
- **Pads and posts:** on a tablet they carry their words. On a phone only today's is
  marked, and the line names it.

## The behaviour bank

Six themes. A word gets one in three ways:
1. the person taps what their word means to them;
2. otherwise a lexicon of about 120 words decides;
3. otherwise a seeded gentle default.

Every word works and looks intended.

| Theme | Koi | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest** (abide, peace, still) | Long idles in the shade of a yes pad, sinking deep. | Hangs low and loose, the slowest sway, with jasmine. |
| **Together** (kind, welcome, love) | Follows a companion, a different one each day. | A tendril reaches over and twines with its neighbour. |
| **Growing** (brave, learn, bold) | Explores, leads, crosses the whole pond. | The longest branch, leaning up towards the sun. |
| **Shining** (joy, hope, thankful) | Rises to the surface often and catches the light. | Covered in blossom that opens wider in the sun. |
| **Giving** (generous, serve, help) | Visits each other koi in turn. | Bears a grape cluster that ripens through the season. |
| **Steadfast** (steady, faithful, rooted) | Keeps a slow, even round of the still water. | The thickest branch, which barely bends in a gust. |

Variation comes from:
- per-person seeds;
- schedules timed on unrelated clocks;
- a companion that changes daily;
- each koi's own temperament;
- wind built from three incommensurate waves;
- the clock and the season.

## The switch

- **The family chooses** the board's world at the end of the ritual.
- **Any device can show the other one.** A quiet mark in the corner opens a two-tile
  sheet (*this tablet shows*), and the choice is remembered per device.
- **It carries through** the home and idle screens, each person's board, the ritual's
  finale, the season page and the paper's palette.
- **The prayer stays paper.**

## Rejected

- A list board with a hero picture: the failure we're fixing.
- A behaviour hand-made for each word.
- Dandelion clocks for "no". Their seeds spread, so the picture says the nos multiply.
  Pruning is the vine's own holy no (bench).
- Koi carrying words, and names following fish: moving labels that land over words.
- A no for each child shown to the family. The bench said this becomes public
  confession or accusation; children get a yes only.
- Fruit for each verse, per person: a trophy count. Growth belongs to the family and
  follows the weeks lived.
- "Is it one of our nos?" as a decision test. Replaced by *which way leaves us more
  loving and more at peace?*
- A third world (sky). The vine is the better second world, and two done well beat
  three done thinly.
- A space-colonisation tree. It is beautiful, but generic; the vine writes the word,
  and John 15 gives the branches their meaning.

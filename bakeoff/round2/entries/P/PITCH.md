# Round 2, entry P: Our Way

**The board, in one sentence:**
Our way for this season (one word for who we're becoming, a few yeses, and the no each
yes protects) lives in a world you leave on the side: a pond of koi, or a vine in a
field.

**Every day.** The board is the home screen and the idle screen. A glance gives three
things:
- **The word.** It lies in the pond's still water, or the vine writes it.
- **One line for today.** *Today: yes to slow dinners together.* The yeses take turns;
  a no is never shown here.
- **Who we pray for.**

Nothing to tick, no streak, no count. Each day one small thing is new: on the vine, a
tendril finds today's post; in the pond, a water lily opens on today's pad. Touch your
koi and it follows your finger; touch your branch and it lifts towards you. Either way,
your name and word rest there for a moment.

*Weighing a decision?* is one quiet link. It holds the decision up to the board:
- Does it help us be *Rooted*?
- Does it make room for one of our yeses?
- Which way leaves us more loving and more at peace?

It keeps nothing.

**Each term.** Pass the tablet:
1. **Look back.** We start by asking where we saw God this term. Nothing is marked kept
   or broken.
2. **Each person in turn.**
   - They see their own creature first ("Pip, this is you").
   - They tap a picture of what they want to grow in.
   - They tap a word that fits it, so a child never has to spell; an older one can type
     their own.
   - They choose one yes. A grown-up may add a no, which stays on their own board.
3. **Together.** The family's word, its yeses and the no each protects are chosen from
   what everyone brought. That is how personal boards feed the family's.
4. **The world.** Then where the board will live.
5. **The finale.**
   - The paper lifts, and the youngest touches the world to let our word in.
   - Each of us arrives by name: a koi swims in, or a branch grows.
   - Each no is let go: a stone sinks to rest, or a cutting falls from the vine.

**The worlds.**
- **The pond** is best at presence and being known. Each of us is a koi, moving the way
  our word means: resting under a yes pad, following a companion, exploring, rising into
  the light, visiting each other, or keeping a steady round. Every few minutes the koi
  gather round the word.
- **The vine** is best at growth and belonging. "I am the vine, you are the branches."
  - The vine, unnamed, rises from the soil and writes our word.
  - Each person is a branch shaped by their word.
  - The yeses are the trellis that holds it up, and each no is a pruning.
  - The fruit ripens with the season, never with effort.
- **The switch.** The family chooses the world in the ritual. Any screen can show the
  other (a quiet mark in the corner), and it remembers. The world carries through the
  home and idle screens, each person's board, the finale, after Amen, and the paper's
  palette. The prayer stays paper.

**Kept, cut and borrowed.**
- **Kept:** Quiet Waters entire (the prayer, the puzzle, confession), the living koi, and
  the old vine word-writer, now rooted and given branches.
- **Cut:**
  - Koi carrying names, so nothing ever crosses the words.
  - Half the drifting leaves.
  - The full verse on the phone glance; the reference is a tap from the verse.
  - The family line on a phone.
  - Any no shown for a child.
- **Borrowed:**
  - Table's "tap who's here" spirit: a creature first, then pictures.
  - Abide's sky, one wind, clouds and grass.

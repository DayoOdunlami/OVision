Read `CLAUDE.md` first: it holds this project's standards and applies to every agent, not
only Claude. The skills it mentions live in `.claude/skills/`; read the `SKILL.md` files
directly.

letter, read `bakeoff/BRIEF.md` and `bakeoff/RULES.md` before anything else. In the
bake-off the house skills are the current studio's standards, which you may adopt, adapt
or overturn with reasons.
